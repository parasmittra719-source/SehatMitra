SehatMitra 3-Minute Demo Script

(0:00 – 0:20) Introduction
[Camera/Voiceover]: "Hello, I’m [Name], and this is SehatMitra, an offline AI health assistant for rural India. We address a critical problem: 70% of Indians lack reliable internet, cutting them off from vital healthcare information."
[Visual]: Slide showing "Problem: Healthcare info gap in rural India."

(0:20 – 0:40) Problem Story
[Narrative]: "Imagine a farmer in a remote village feeling unwell with a high fever. He can’t easily travel to a clinic and has no internet to search for advice. SehatMitra lets him talk to an AI in Hindi, completely offline, to get immediate, grounded guidance."
[Visual]: Map of India highlighting rural areas; statistic on screen.

(0:40 – 0:50) Solution Overview
[Narrative]: "SehatMitra runs locally on device. It uses Gemma 4’s advanced multilingual model to understand Hindi and cross-references queries with a local database of vetted health guidelines."
[Visual]: System Architecture diagram showing User -> Flask API -> Gemma 4 + Local DB.

(0:50 – 1:50) Live Demo
[Action]: Screen-share the SehatMitra web UI.
[Narrative]: "Let's see it in action. I'll type a common symptom: 'मुझे बुखार और सर दर्द है' (I have a fever and headache)."
[Action]: Click 'Ask'. Show the typing indicator.
[Narrative]: "The system processes this offline. It retrieves WHO guidelines from its local database and generates a response."
[Action]: Response appears with the "Source: Vetted Guidelines" badge.
[Narrative]: "Notice the answer is clear, in Hindi, and clearly states it's based on vetted sources. It advises rest, hydration, and when to see a doctor."

(1:50 – 2:20) Backend Peek
[Action]: Switch to terminal showing Flask logs.
[Narrative]: "Here’s a quick look under the hood. You can see the Flask backend receiving the query, performing the local retrieval, and calling the Gemma 4 pipeline to generate the response."

(2:20 – 2:40) Impact & Wow Factor
[Narrative]: "Unlike generic cloud chatbots, SehatMitra is designed specifically for low-resource environments. By combining Gemma 4's capabilities with local retrieval, we ensure safety and reliability without needing an internet connection."
[Visual]: Highlight the "Offline-First" badge in the UI.

(2:40 – 2:55) Wrap-Up
[Narrative]: "Built in just 16 hours by a single developer, this prototype proves the viability of on-device AI for social good. Our next steps include adding voice I/O and expanding the local knowledge base."

(2:55 – 3:00) Call to Action
